# Tax & Audit Tools

[English](README.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

I build open-source tools for tax screening, reporting preparation and financial reconciliation. My focus is making assumptions visible and helping reviewers trace a result back to its inputs.

These projects combine domain-focused workflows with AI-assisted development. They are prototypes with documented limits. I welcome reproducible examples, questions about the methods and feedback on day-to-day usability.

## Help improve one workflow

Choose one fictional case and change one important input. Tell us what you expected, what happened and which step was unclear. For a rule question, include the relevant period and an official source. Reports are reviewed weekly.

Feedback belongs in the relevant project’s Issues. English, German and Chinese are welcome.

Use fictional data in public demos and Issues. The project hub has no analytics or form submissions. GitHub Pages records ordinary visitor information under GitHub’s privacy policy. A working demo is not a professional conclusion or filing service.
