Practical tools for tax and audit workflows.

I build open-source tools for tax screening, reporting preparation and financial reconciliation. My focus is making assumptions visible and helping reviewers trace a result back to its inputs.

Explore projects

About this work

These projects combine domain-focused workflows with AI-assisted development. They are prototypes with documented limits. I welcome reproducible examples, questions about the methods and feedback on day-to-day usability.

Pillar Two / Mindeststeuer

Pillar 2 Navigator

v2 · Prototype

Pillar Two obligation screening

Review German scope, transitional CbCR Safe Harbour conditions and separate filing prompts in English, German or Chinese.

Standard calendar years 2024 to 2026; inputs in EUR millions. No top-up tax calculation or filing.

Reconciliation / Debitorenabstimmung

AR Reconciliation Workbench

v0.4 · Prototype

Open items, GL and statements

Reconcile accounts receivable through a browser-local workflow and export a German or English Excel snapshot.

The full accounts-payable workflow is not validated. Matching totals do not establish audit completion.

A one-minute first look
  1. Open the tool. Download examples/synthetic-project.json from its repository.
  2. Use Open project to load it, then inspect the analysis and remaining review items.
  3. Preview the export and identify one missing control or confusing label.
